# Scalability

`cloud` `fundamentals`

Your ability to scale up or down, depending on your budget, traffic, available memory, or other resources you want to track.

Types of scaling:
- Vertical (Scaling up): Improving existing server by replacing it with a higher-resources one (Better RAM, Disk capacity, etc)
- Horizontal (Scaling out) - adding separate servers to compliment your existing servers.
