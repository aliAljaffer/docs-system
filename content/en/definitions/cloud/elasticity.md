# elasticity

The ability to ***AUTOMATICALLY*** increase or decrease your resources based on metrics you are tracking.

Utilize a service such as [[autoscaling-groups-and-load-balancers|Auto Scaling Groups]] to ***AUTOMATICALLY*** add or remove resources.
