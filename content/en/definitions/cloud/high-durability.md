# high durability

Ability to recover from disaster with minimal data lost

## RTO **Recovery Time Objective**
(RTO) me your business can afford to be offline due to an incident without incurring a substantial loss. (MEASURE OF TIME) #rto
## RPO **Recovery Point Objective**
(RPO) - Max amount of data that can be lost due to an incident.  (MEASURE OF DATA) #rpo
