# Vpn

`aws` `services`

## Virtual Private Networks (VPN)
A VPN lets you establish a secure private tunnel between your on premise network and personal devices. 

**Vs.**

An **AWS VPN** establishes the same tunnel but between your network/devices and the AWS global network.
### VPN Types
- Site-to-Site - connect on-premise networks to [[vpc|VPC]]
- Client VPN - Connect end users to AWS or on-premise networks.
