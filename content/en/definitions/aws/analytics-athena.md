# athena

`aws` `services`

Amazon Athena is a [[serverless]] SQL like service that is primarily used to analyze data stored in [[storage-s3|S3]]. Queries can be performed in a variety of standards, including CSV, JSON, ORC, Avro, and Parquet. Queries can also be executed in parallel, resulting in extremely high performance
