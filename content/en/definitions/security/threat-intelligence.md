# Threat intelligence

Cyberthreat intelligence is data that an organization has collected and analyzed to understand the motives and behavior of threat actors. It focuses on **Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures** (TTPs) and **Indicators of Compromise** (IoCs).

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Tactics | The why: the adversary's goal | Increase privileges |
| Techniques | The how: ways to reach the goal | Bypass access controls |
| Procedures | The specific implementation | A particular tool or program |
| IoCs | Signatures of attacker activity | IP addresses linked to a threat group, or certain files |

The presence of an IoC can indicate that an organization is already compromised.

## Benefits

1. A warning.
2. IoCs.
3. Context.
4. Learning from peers.
