# cryptography

...is the principles, means, and methods of transforming data to conceal its content, prevent its unauthorized use, or prevent its undetected modification.

## Symmetric Encryption
Same key used for encryption and decryption. Fast and easy. Both sender and receiver must know the key.

Commonly used algorithm: Advanced Encryption Standard (AES)
## Asymmetric Encryption
use public-private keys that are generated simultaneously. Public key can be shared to anyone.
## Quantum-safe encryption
Encryption methods that are resistant to quantum computing attacks.
1. Lattice-based encryption: uses multidimensional geometric structures to encrypt data
2. Hash-based encryption
3. Multivariate encryption
4. Code-based encryption: Transforms data into encoded messages that are difficult to decode without the correct algorithm.
