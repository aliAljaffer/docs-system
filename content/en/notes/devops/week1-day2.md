## Agile
Agile is a mindset rather than a methodology. 
#### Four Core Values of Agile
1. **Individuals and interactions** over processes and tools
2. **Working software** over comprehensive documentation
3. **Customer collaboration** over contract negotiation
4. **Responding to change** over following a plan
### Twelve Principles of Agile
1. **Early and continuous delivery of value**
2. **Welcome change, even late in the project**
3. **Deliver working software frequently**
4. **Daily collaboration between business and developers**
5. **Build projects around motivated individuals**
6. **Working product is the primary measure of progress**
7. **Sustainable pace**
8. **Continuous attention to technical excellence**
9. **Simplicity – maximize the work not done
10. **Self-organizing teams
11. **Regular reflection and adjustment

## Scrum Framework
### Three roles
- **Scrum Master**: Identifies and removes impediments, helps developers raise concerns to org, guides team to scrum practices.
- **Product Owner**: Maintains the product backlog and its prioritization
- **Developers**: The team that develops Increments, creates Sprint Backlog from Product Backlog
### Five Events
1. **Sprint Planning:** Why is the sprint valuable? What can we get Done this sprint? How will work get Done? **this produces a Sprint Backlog with a Sprint Goal**
2. **Sprint**: Usually a 2-week timeboxed event where work gets done. **This produces a Done increment**
3. **Daily Scrum**: A 15-minute (regardless of Sprint timebox) event where teams discuss what they did yesterday, what they'll do today, and any blockers.
4. **Sprint Review**: The team present the increment to stakeholders, usually a demo.
5. **Sprint Retrospective**: The team reflect on the previous Sprint, what went well, wrong, and what can be improved for the next Sprint.
### Three Artifacts
- Product Backlog & Product Goal
- Sprint Backlog & Sprint Goal
- Increment & Definition of Done
